\begin{abstract}
We give a finite incidence construction for one-sided inverses in group algebras of torsion-free groups in every odd prime characteristic. Projective lines supply the required intersection congruences; Hadamard blocks provide regular auxiliary incidences; and unions of concurrent lines correct inverse-letter balances exactly over the integers. The repair-only union rows have fixed multiplicities, leaving a replicated bulk of lines and Hadamard-decorated lines whose reduced-turn kernel admits a uniform spectral bound. If $p\ge5$ and a real Hadamard matrix of order $4p$ exists, a plane of order $p^3$ gives $p^6+p^3+4p$ signed letters; Du--Jiang's existence theorem gives at most $p^6+p^3+2^{10}p^{23/20}$ for $p\ge2^{23}$. In characteristic three we specify a $6654$-letter input with root degree $93$ and maximum graph degree $325$. The signed-matching and bounded-path methods of OpenAI and Ivanov, together with Mghirbi--Douglas's cone criterion, realize these data in finitely presented torsion-free word-hyperbolic groups, with a finite classifying complex of dimension at most two and witnesses $ab=1$, $ac=0$, $c\ne0$. The contribution is a quantitative refinement of the finite input to the qualitative group-algebra result of Mghirbi--Douglas.
\end{abstract}
